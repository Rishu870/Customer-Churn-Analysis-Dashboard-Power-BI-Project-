# Customer-Churn-Analysis-Dashboard-Power-BI-Project-
Customer Churn Analysis Dashboard built using Power BI to identify key factors influencing customer attrition. The project focuses on analyzing customer behavior, calculating churn metrics, and generating actionable insights to improve retention and business performance.

Problem Statement

Customer churn is a major challenge for businesses as losing customers directly impacts revenue and growth. Companies need to identify why customers are leaving and take proactive actions to improve retention. The goal of this project is to analyze customer data and identify key factors contributing to churn using data visualization and business intelligence techniques.

##  Solution Approach

This project uses Power BI to transform raw customer data into actionable insights through:

* Data cleaning and preprocessing
* KPI identification (Churn Rate, Total Customers, Revenue Loss)
* Interactive dashboards for trend analysis
* Visual representation of customer behavior patterns

The dashboard helps stakeholders understand churn drivers and take data-driven decisions.
##  Tools & Technologies

* Power BI (Dashboard & Visualization)
* SQL (Data Extraction & Querying)
* Excel / CSV (Dataset)
* DAX (Calculated Measures & KPIs)

* ## 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

* Total Customers
* Churned Customers
* Churn Rate (%)
* Revenue Lost due to Churn
##  Key Insights

* High churn observed in specific customer segments
* Customers with shorter tenure are more likely to churn
* Certain services/products show higher churn rates
* Monthly trends indicate patterns in customer drop-offs

These insights can help businesses improve customer retention strategies.

## 🚀 Business Recommendations

* Focus on early customer engagement to reduce churn
* Provide targeted offers to high-risk customers
* Improve service quality in high-churn categories
* Use predictive analytics for proactive retention
